Read the root `AGENTS.md` and `docs/production/SUPPORT_AND_TRIAGE.md`. Classify the report, deduplicate it, identify missing build/save/runtime/reproduction evidence, and decide whether it is only support, a compatibility question, a feature request, collaboration, or a real bug candidate. Do not edit code. Do not turn vague community reports into patches. If a concrete bug exists, prepare a bounded BUGS/WORK_QUEUE-ready contract and recommend OpenCode or Codex according to the documented escalation boundaries.
