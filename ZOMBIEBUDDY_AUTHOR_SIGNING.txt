KNOX SURVIVORS - ZOMBIEBUDDY AUTHOR / TRUST SETUP

IMPORTANT
The normal Project Zomboid mod metadata already identifies the author as:
  author=.exe

ZombieBuddy's approval/trust UI is different. It does NOT use the mod.info author= field as cryptographic author identity.
To replace "Unknown Author" with a verifiable ZombieBuddy author identity, sign the FINAL Knox JAR using ZombieBuddy's ZBS signing system.

What a signed release contains:
  knox-agent.jar
  knox-agent.jar.zbs

The .zbs file is generated from YOUR private Ed25519 key and YOUR SteamID64.
Do not share or commit the private key. Regenerate the .zbs sidecar every time the JAR changes.

Official ZombieBuddy signing flow:
1. Generate an Ed25519 private key on your own PC.
2. Configure your SteamID64 and private-key path in your local Gradle properties / ZombieBuddy signing setup.
3. Sign the final Knox JAR.
4. Put knox-agent.jar.zbs next to knox-agent.jar in the released mod.
5. Publish the generated public key on your Steam profile as:
     JavaModZBS:<public-key>
   OR request that ZombieBuddy add your SteamID64, display name (.exe), and public key to its authors.json.
6. Rebuild/re-sign every changed Knox JAR before release.

Why this ZIP does not include knox-agent.jar.zbs:
A valid signature must be created with your private key and SteamID64. Packaging a fake/generic signature would defeat the trust system.
